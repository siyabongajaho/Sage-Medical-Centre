Caring for the whole family

Sage Medical Centre is a general practice in Reitzpark, Welkom, led by Dr Nkomo. We see adults and children for everyday illness, long-term conditions and minor procedures.

Need a repeat script, a specialist referral or a medical report? We handle that too, and our on-site dispensary means you can collect your medication before you head home.

We accept cash and most medical aids.

Our mission

To give every patient in Reitzpark and Welkom professional, caring and accessible healthcare, from everyday illness to ongoing conditions, with respect and dignity at every visit.

Our vision

To be the community's trusted family practice, inspiring better health for generations to come.
